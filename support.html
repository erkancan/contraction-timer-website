Frequently asked

  • My Apple Watch and iPhone show different data. Open the app on both devices while they are within Bluetooth range; syncing resumes automatically.
  • How do I export my history? Go to the Report tab and use the share button to generate a PDF of your contractions and kick sessions.
  • How do I manage or cancel my subscription? Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open the Settings app → tap your name → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.
  • How do I restore a purchase on a new device? Open More → Settings → Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • How do I delete my data? All data is stored on your device — deleting the app removes it permanently.

Important

This app is a tracking tool, not a medical device, and does not provide medical advice. If you are worried about your labour or your baby's movements, contact your midwife, doctor or emergency services immediately.
